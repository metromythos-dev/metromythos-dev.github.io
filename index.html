About

I built my career in two disciplines that turn out to have a lot in common. For more than a decade I worked as an Executive Sous Chef, Chef and Event Planner, and Banquet Chef, where I learned how a business really runs: the pace of service, what makes a product worth talking about, and what customers respond to when they say a place "feels right."

Since 2018, I have applied that instinct to digital marketing. I have built social strategy, websites, paid campaigns, and email automation for restaurants, a multi-concept restaurant group, a historic hotel, and most recently a luxury landscape and hardscape brand serving homeowners and B2B trade partners.

Across every client, the approach is the same: understand the product well enough to market it honestly, test what works, and show up consistently until people notice.

Two

Crocante Kitchen + Bar

Dec 2022 to Aug 2025
Digital Marketer (Self-Employed)

One of my most successful engagements, and the first where I had complete creative control. Crocante started as a food truck, and I worked with ownership on a grand-opening campaign built partly around that origin story, which produced a highly successful opening for the brick-and-mortar restaurant.

From there, I built a content calendar, owned copywriting and graphic design, and pushed for a video-first approach that grew the Facebook page from about 5,000 to 42,000+ followers, mostly through organic strategy. I grew reach further by engaging with other Orlando food pages, and linked Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok directly to the restaurant's online reservations, connecting social growth to real bookings.

Proof point: testing whether ads were really driving revenue

Crocante's volume was growing, but it was still spending heavily on Meta ads for brand awareness. I designed an incrementality test that scaled ad spend back in planned increments over three months while tracking revenue closely.

Result: the restaurant kept growing its clientele as spend went down, saving thousands of dollars in marketing costs without sacrificing business.
